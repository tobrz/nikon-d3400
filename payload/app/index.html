Lightning

    
Sprays the shutter, then copies the new frames off the card. A digital light sensor can be added later on GPIO 17. USB is slower than the bolt, so leave the camera in manual focus and manual exposure first.

Known networks are tried first. If none are in range, the Pi is an access point at 192.168.50.1 and hands your phone an address. Saving a home network from this access point will make the Pi leave it.
